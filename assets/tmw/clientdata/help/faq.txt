##3                      === <<FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS>> ===


##3 <<##BBeginner Stats##b>>
 <<##9If you never played before, make a build based on agility and dexterity, with
 some luck and strength. Agility makes you survive, Dex makes you hit enemies,
 Luck increases drop rates and Strength makes you hit harder.>>


##3 <<##BDealing with Lag##b>>
 <<##9To deal with lag, please use ##B##3@resyncall##9##b and it will resync
 everything on your screen, including the weather.>>
 <<##9If you're an archer, in settings -> misc, disable server-side attack, disable
 auto fix position, and in settings -> quick tab, third option, set to archer attack
 range.>>


##3 <<##BGetting Money##b>>
 <<##9##BAlways:##b Try completing quests. Specially daily quests. They provide
 the best income at Candor.>>
 <<##9##BEarly Game:##b Sell Bat Wings and Coin Bag on the shop.>>
 <<##9##BMid Game:##b Sell Dragon Scales on the shop.>>
 <<##9##BEnd Game##b: Sell Crafty Wings on the shop.>>

 <<##9##BCandor##b: Getting stuck without money on Candor after completing all
 quests may lock your game, as it will take a long time to get money with Candor
 drops.>>
 <<##9##BTulimshar##b: Get a fishing rod with Eugene. You need to find a secret
 passage to reach him, on the very centre of Tulimshar, isolated on his own island.
 Hint: You cannot swim.>>

 <<##9##BShip Prices##b: Travel prices will go down the more quests you complete on
 destination and current location. Keep in mind that except Candor, you can walk
 on foot to anywhere on the world, without needing to pay.>>


##3 <<##BGetting Experience##b>>
 <<##9##BQuests:##b The main quest provide the best experience rewards, and it also
 unlocks useful features, like Homunculus.>>

 <<##9##BWithout grinding:##b If you don't want to kill monsters, you also get
 experience by crafting and fishing. The easiest place to get experience without
 fighting is inside Tulimshar Magic Council. In the red rug with the Professor,
 you can get experience by staying still.>>

 <<##9##BBoosters:##b Alcoholic drinks, like Beer, Wine, Sake, Rum and Whiskey,
 for no specific reason, acts as Experience Boosters. Their effects expire on
 death and after a certain time has elapsed. It goes from ten minutes to three
 hours depending on how strong is the drink. The more players are around you when
 you drink, the bigger the experience boost will be.>>

 <<##9##BGrinding:##b Early game, hunt Giant Maggots. Midgame, switch to Trolls
 and/or Black Mambas. Late game, Whirly Birds and heroes hold -boss dungeon give
 the most.>>


##3 <<##BGetting magic and skills##b>>
 <<##9You can learn basic tricks at Tulimshar Magic School. However, to obtain
 magic, you must find and touch the Mana Stone. If you're not strong enough, you
 will fail.>>
 <<##9The main quest should give you the opportunity to do so, but you won't be
 shoved at learning magic.>>
 <<##9Do note you're not a super-human/super-elf/super-ukar/etc - All skills,
 including physical ones, are considered magic and will use mana points.>>
